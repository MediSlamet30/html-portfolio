The Best Anime According to Medi

My top 3 anime of all-time.

One Piece

This is one of my favourite anime. I love the adventure, the friendship between the Straw Hat Pirates, and Luffy's dream of becoming the Pirate King.

Black Clover

I love this anime because of Asta's determination to become the Wizard King, even though he cannot use magic.

Hunter X Hunter

This is an amazing anime with exciting adventures, unique characters, and incredible battles.
